## Upload defaults (Settings > Upload defaults)

- Category: Film & Animation. Language: English. Caption certification: none.
- Tags: the keyword list above. Licence: Standard YouTube Licence.
- Comments: on, "hold potentially inappropriate comments for review".
- Audience ("made for kids"): this is your legal call under COPPA. The show is written for a general,
  adult-leaning audience (lofi, history, exam stress, no characters aimed at children), so "No, it's not made
  for kids" fits; if you pick "Yes", comments, notifications and the end screens below stop working.
- Altered or synthetic content: not needed. The label is for realistic footage that could be mistaken for real
  people or events; this is stylised pixel animation, and the music is generated by code in this repo (no
  samples, no AI audio, nothing for Content ID to match).

## The look, in one paragraph (for anyone making extra art)

320×180 pixel world, scaled ×6 (1080p) with nearest neighbour; text in GNU Unifont. Warm interior
(lantern orange, wood brown, tiled kitchen cream) against indigo night and rain. Brand colours: indigo
`#1E2242`, bowl red `#B42C24`, naruto pink `#E85C80`, cream `#FCF0D6`, gold `#FFC45C`, ink `#1A1220`.
