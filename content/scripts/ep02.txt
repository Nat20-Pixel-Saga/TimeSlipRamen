kon>mika: Who did you get wrong?
mika>kon: Every samurai. They all have three names and none of them ever turns up on time.

> prop cards seat=1 dx=-13

> chapter Musashi
> cho eye=wide look=-2,1

> short 3 start
haru: Irasshaimase! ...We close in five minutes.
musashi: Then I am right on time. I am always late.
haru: That's not how time works.
musashi: It is how duels work. Make the other man wait. He grows angry. Angry men make mistakes.
> short 3 end
haru: I'm not your opponent. I'm your cook.
musashi: ...Then I apologise. Partly.
> short 3 start
> card musashi_duel
mika: Miyamoto Musashi? The duel on the island?
musashi>mika: You know of it?
mika>musashi: You're the one samurai I remember.
musashi>mika: Because I won?
mika>musashi: Because you were late.
> short 3 end
> serve musashi shoyu
> prop oar seat=4 dx=-2 shavings=1
musashi: Itadakimasu.
> eat musashi 5 2
kon>musashi: Is that an oar?
musashi>kon: On the boat it was an oar. On the island it was a sword. Tonight it is an oar again.
gen: Cosplay.
musashi>haru: What did he say?
haru: He says your costume is very good.
musashi: It is not a costume. It is my only shirt.
> card musashi_book
mika>musashi: You wrote a whole book? And painted birds?
musashi: In the end, everything is one way. The sword, the brush, the noodles.
> slurp musashi
musashi: ...Mostly the noodles.
haru: Would you sign the wall?
> sign musashi
> unprop oar
musashi: I will come back. Late.
> leave musashi to=sea
> finish musashi
> clear musashi
> wait 1.5
> chapter Tanuki
> cho eye=wide look=-2,1

> short 4 start

tanuki: Good evening! I am a completely normal human customer.
kon>tanuki: You're a tanuki.
tanuki>kon: I am a businessman. From the city. With a tail.

> short 4 start
> transform tanuki kettle 7
> set tanuki wobble 1.0 7

> sfx pour
